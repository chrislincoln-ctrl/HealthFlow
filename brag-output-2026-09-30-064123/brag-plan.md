# Brag Plan: HealthFlow — Hero Teaser

## What is this app?
HealthFlow is a predictive health-network intelligence prototype: it watches a synthetic PHC
supply network, forecasts where shortages will hit, ranks the risk, and recommends resource
transfers before patients feel the gap.

## The angle
Not a walkthrough — a **brand film**. The teaser is a premium cinematic object designed to live
inside the EMPTY RIGHT HALF of the existing landing-page hero. The system THINKS and RESPONDS:
a quiet network wakes → one point risks failure → intelligence finds the surplus → resource
flow travels the corridors → the network stabilizes → the identity resolves. Conceptual arc:
**RISK → INTELLIGENCE → RESOURCE FLOW → RESILIENCE**. Abstract logistics + geographic network
intelligence + elegant data visualization; zero stock footage, zero dashboards, zero district
names, zero fabricated numbers.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Darkness with atmosphere. A handful of facility nodes breathe into existence out of soft
forest-green fog while faint connection lines begin drawing between them — the viewer
instantly understands "a living network" without a single word.

## Key moments (the middle)
- One node develops a slow red pulse; the risk visibly PROPAGATES along its corridors.
- A violet intelligence sweep emanates outward; warm surplus points illuminate.
- Resource-flow paths travel visibly along curved corridors from surplus → vulnerable point.
- The red halo dissolves; the node settles to healthy green; the whole net calms.

## Outro / punchline
The network resolves into a calm constellation, the ECG line draws through, and
**HEALTHFLOW** locks in with the triad tagline. Final soft fade so the hero loop restarts
imperceptibly.

## User flow worth showing
None — this is a brand film, not a UI tour (the previous /brag covered the product loop).
The "show the thing" law is satisfied by the network-map visual language the product itself
uses (nodes, corridors, risk halo, flow paths) rendered cinematically.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Atmospheric network reveal — 0.0–4.2s (4.2s)
Dark forest-green atmosphere, drifting soft particles, slow 1.06→1.0 camera settle. Eleven
facility nodes fade/scale in gently (staggered), thin corridor lines draw between neighbors.
Sequential: nodes arrive one by one. Text: none.
Audio intent: the bed fades in under fog; a sense of a system waking.
Audio-coupled idea: none. Music: low cinematic bed. Transition: soft crossfade → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Risk emerges — 4.2–8.0s (3.8s)
One node ignites a slow red pulse; a red halo blooms; connected corridors dim toward risk
tones as the signal propagates outward. "PREDICT SHORTAGES." lands large, upper-left, 4.6s,
holds ~2.6s. Sequential: halo → corridor tint → phrase.
Audio intent: tension enters quietly. Audio-coupled: drop_001 at halo bloom.
Music: bed continues. Transition: soft → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Intelligence & resource flow — 8.0–12.4s (4.4s)
A violet intelligence sweep ripples outward from center (the THINKING moment); warm peach
surplus nodes illuminate; teal-green flow paths travel visibly along curved corridors into
the vulnerable point; its red halo begins to shrink. "MOVE RESOURCES." lands at 8.74s
(beat-locked), holds ~2.8s. Sequential: sweep → surplus lights → paths travel.
Audio intent: quiet resolve — the system responds. Audio-coupled: bong_001 at sweep.
Music: bed. Transition: soft → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Stabilization — 12.4–15.4s (3.0s)

settle; the net feels balanced. "PROTECT CARE." lands 12.6s, holds ~2.4s.
Audio intent: exhale. Audio-coupled: none. Music: bed. Transition: gentle push → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Brand resolution — 15.4–18.0s (2.6s)

Music: fades out 17.2→17.9.
